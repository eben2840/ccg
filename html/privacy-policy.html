Privacy Policy

Privacy Notice

Coastline Citadel Group

This privacy notice was last reviewed or revised on January 4, 2026.

We, Coastline Citadel Group, our subsidiaries, and affiliates (collectively, "Coastline Citadel," "us," or "we") understand that your privacy is important to you. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting your personal data. This privacy notice describes how we handle and protect your personal data (the "Privacy Notice") when we collect it through Coastline Citadel's websites, applications, and digital assets (collectively, our "Sites") and through our externally facing business activities, such as service offerings, events, surveys, and communications.

1. Data Controller

When Coastline Citadel collects and processes your personal data, we do so as authorize under applicable data privacy laws, acting as a data controller. This means we determine and are responsible for how your personal data is collected, used, protected, and disclosed.

2. How Do We Collect Your Personal Data?

Coastline Citadel collects personal data during our business activities directly from you and from third parties:

  • Direct Interactions: When you register for newsletters, create a user profile on our Sites, sign up for conferences, or interact with our employees via email or telephone.
  • Automated Technologies: As you interact with our Sites, we may automatically collect technical data about your equipment and browsing patterns via cookies and similar tools.
  • Third Parties: We may receive professional information from service providers or public sources (such as business websites and professional social media platforms) to ensure our records are accurate and relevant.

3. Why and How Are We Using Your Personal Data?

Purpose Categories of Data Legal Basis
Managing Relationships Name, email, job title, company. Legitimate interest in providing client services.
Newsletters & Alerts Name, email, professional interests. Consent or legitimate interest in marketing.
Conferences & Events Contact details, dietary requirements, professional role. Legitimate interest in event management; Consent for media.
Benchmarking & Analytics Industry data, behavioral data, IP addresses. Legitimate interest in improving our service offerings.
Legal compliance As required by specific legal mandates. Compliance with legal obligations.

4. Who Has Access to Your Personal Data?

Personal data may be transferred to Coastline Citadel entities, service providers, and third parties as necessary for the business purposes described above. We do not sell your personal data.

We may disclose your information:

  • To our global subsidiaries and affiliates.
  • To service providers performing functions on our behalf (e.g., IT support, mail houses).
  • If required by law, legal process, or to protect the vital interests of any person.

5. International Data Transfers

As a global organization, personal data may be transferred and stored outside of your country of residence. Coastline Citadel complies with applicable data transfer laws by incorporating standard contractual clauses or similar legal mechanisms into our agreements to ensure your data remains protected.

6. Security

Coastline Citadel has implemented generally accepted standards of technical and operational security to protect personal data from loss, misuse, alteration, or destruction. Only authorized personnel are granted access to personal data, and they are required to treat this information as confidential.

7. Data Retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

8. Your Data Protection Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we amend inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we erase your personal data.
  • Withdraw Consent: Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time.
  • Portability: Request the transfer of your data to another party.

9. Contact Us

To exercise your rights or if you have questions about this policy, please contact our Data Protection Office:
